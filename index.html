A Journey to Remember

My favorite vacation destination is Barcelona, Spain. I visited the summer after graduating high school with my family. What started as a trip abroad became one of the most meaningful experiences of my life.

The architecture, history, atmosphere, and culture made Spain unforgettable. Most importantly, this trip brought me closer to my parents and reminded me of my grandmother, who loved beautiful churches and stained glass.

ART MEETS HISTORY

Architecture That Tells a Story

The Sagrada Família is unlike anything I had ever seen. Its intricate architecture and biblical sculptures represent different moments in religious history. Every corner has something unique to discover.

One of my favorite experiences was climbing one of the towers and seeing Barcelona from above.

LIGHT, COLOR & MEMORY

A Little Piece of Magic

The stained glass windows were one of the most beautiful parts of the entire experience. When the sunlight shines through, the colors transform the inside of the basilica into something almost otherworldly.

My grandmother loved stained glass and church bells. Seeing these beautiful details made me feel connected to her, even though she was no longer with us.

More Than Just Barcelona

We also explored Seville, experiencing more of Spain's culture, architecture, weather, and atmosphere. I especially loved learning about the tradition of siestas. We were initially confused when shops and businesses closed in the afternoon!

Traveling through Spain taught me how to navigate unfamiliar places, appreciate different cultures, and enjoy slowing down.

Why It Will Always Be Special

The Sagrada Família is still under construction, continuing a vision that began generations ago. It reminds me that meaningful things take time, dedication, and creativity.

I believe we are missing character and whimsy in modern architecture. So many buildings today are designed to be simple and inexpensive, but there is something extraordinary about creating something imaginative and beautiful that lasts beyond your own lifetime.

"We're missing character and whimsy in our lives."

Barcelona, Spain

A trip, a memory, and a place I'll never forget.
